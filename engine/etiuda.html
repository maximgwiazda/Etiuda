  Click any macro to copy just that macro - cards badged 1/4, 2/4… are alternatives, copied one at a time. {PAX} fills from the name box; {AGENT} and {INIT} from the agent name; {INTENT} from the side panel, from the chips under the opener when the panel is off, or from what you type. A listed intent follows EN|PL; typed text does not. Typing in SEARCH ranks intents and filters cards in both languages.
  

{TOPIC} is the subject of the chat as a noun phrase: where {INTENT} names an act, this names a thing. {ACTION} is what was DONE, for internal comments. Both are set per intent in its editor.
  

Drag to reorder: intents in the side panel (double-click its title to reset), a card header within its highlight group (intent-linked / favourited / both / regular), or alternative and step macros inside a card (EN and PL stay aligned). Show or hide the panel from the  Menu.
