    INTENT
    

      
      
    

  

  
Click · drag to reorder · Ctrl for several

  







  

  
  

    Created by Maxim Gwiazda © 2026 · Etiuda v · Free and open source under the MIT Licence.
  









  Click any macro to copy just that macro - cards badged 1/4, 2/4… are alternatives and copy one at a time.
  {PAX} fills from the name box. {INTENT} fills from the side panel
  or the chips under the opener (when the panel is off) - or type your own. A listed intent follows the EN/PL toggle; typed text does not.
  {AGENT} and /{INIT} both derive from the agent name. Press / or click the INTENT/MACRO label to toggle search mode (both languages).
  

{TOPIC} is the subject of the chat as a noun phrase - where {INTENT} names an act, this names a thing. {ACTION} is what was DONE, for internal comments. Both are set per intent in its editor.
  

Drag intents in the side panel to reorder (double-click the panel title to reset). Drag a card header to reorder within the same highlight group (intent-linked / favourited / both / regular). Drag alternative/step macros inside a card to reorder them (EN and PL stay aligned). Show or hide the panel from the  Menu.
