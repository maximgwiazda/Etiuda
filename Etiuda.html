  Click any macro to copy just that macro - entries badged 1/4, 2/4… are alternatives and copy one at a time.
  {PAX} fills from the name box. {INTENT} fills from the side panel
  or the chips under the opener (when the panel is off) - or type your own. A listed intent follows the EN/PL toggle; typed text does not.
  {AGENT} and /{INIT} both derive from the agent name. Press / or click the INTENT/MACRO label to toggle macro search (both languages).
  

For internal comments, {ACTION} and {TOPIC} fill from the selected intents - set them per intent in its editor. A macro using them writes the comment itself.
  

Drag intents in the side panel to reorder (double-click the panel title to reset). Drag a card header to reorder within the same highlight group (intent green / favourite yellow / both / regular). Drag alternative/step macros inside a card to reorder them (EN and PL stay aligned). Show or hide the panel from  Settings.
