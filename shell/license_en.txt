Privacy. An organisation's catalogs, and everything its people and its customers write, can be read by the organisation alone: neither Etiuda nor Etiuda Studio sends them to us or to anyone else in a form that can be read. A team that carries its files through our relay instead of a shared folder sends them sealed on its own computers, and the relay cannot open them. It sees only random identifiers, the public keys of the team's devices, which file replaces which, each file's size rounded up and the time it was sent, and the IP address and time of each connection, and so how often each device writes. From the signed pass that comes with the licence and admits the team to the relay, it learns only the lead's public key, when the pass was issued and when it ends, and whether the licence is paid; the pass holds no name, no email address and no payment details. The files are stored in the European Union and may pass, still sealed, through Cloudflare's network elsewhere. When and why the programs connect to us is set out in the privacy notice on etiuda.dev.
